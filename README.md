# Gazebo xLights

A 12-sided garden gazebo modeled in 3D and turned into a **1,525-pixel (2" spacing)** xLights model with 14 ready-made animations (`.fseq`) for playback on a Kulp controller running FPP.

> **Status: nothing here has been tested in xLights or on FPP/Kulp hardware.** Files were generated by script. See [Caveats](#caveats).

## Dimensions and assumptions (inches)
Derived from site measurements and photos. Values marked *assumed* are estimates to verify against the build.
- **Plan:** regular 12-gon. Inside distance post #1 to post #7 (opposite posts, inside faces) = **192"**. Posts are 3.5" deep radially, so post-centre corner-to-corner = 195.5", post-centre circumradius **97.75"**, side length (centre to centre) **50.6"**.
- **Posts:** 12 posts, 2x4 (1.5" x 3.5", 3.5" radial), **71"** tall on the ground. Numbered #1-#12 counterclockwise from above, starting at the entrance. The entrance face is between #12 and #1 (face 12, centred at -90°, the -y side).
- **Top joists:** 2x4 laid flat (1.5") on top of the posts, 71"-72.5". Wall top is **72.5"**. They carry **no pixels** (they would duplicate the 72" rails).
- **Rails:** 1x4 (0.75" x 3.5") centred at **72"** (all 12 faces) and **36"** (11 faces; none on the entrance face). *Assumed:* centred at those heights, in the post-centreline plane.
- **Braces:** 2x4 at 45°, in the rail plane, cut flush to post face and rail underside (parallelograms). Upper set: vertex on post at 56" up to the 72" rail. Lower set: vertex at 20" up to the 36" rail (none on the entrance face). *Assumed* geometry; no pixels go on braces.
- **Roof (open framing, no solid roof):** center ring is a 12-gon with base at **89"**, 3.5" tall (*assumed*), inside **22.5"** and outside **29.5"** flat-to-flat (*assumed* flat-to-flat). 12 rafters (2x4), one over each post, from wall top (72.5") to the ring's outer corner at 89". Rafter length computes to **84.1"**, matching the measured 84".

## Pixel layout
**1,525 pixels (RGB, 4,575 channels), 48 strings.** No pixels on braces or top joists. Pixels face inward (toward the center), on the inner side of each member's centerline. 72" rail pixels sit on the inner face of the top joist/rail stack (2.05" inside the post line, clear of the 3.5"-wide joist); ring pixels are on the ring's underside (z = 88.7"), facing down. Posts and rails are at 2" spacing; the ring has 50 pixels at about 1.7".

`scripts/build_web_assets.py` checks that no pixel position falls inside the framing. Animations are defined by physical position (height, angle), not node order, so they look the same whichever way a unit is wired.

Node order **= wiring order** for the **4-line plan (v4)** in [`docs/physical_layout_v4.md`](docs/physical_layout_v4.md): each of the 4 K4 ports is one contiguous node range. "up" = post base > top or rafter wall end > ring. Face f runs post f to f+1; "72" f2 (3>2)" runs it backwards. (`GAZEBO_LAYOUT=v3 python scripts/xl_model.py` rebuilds the previous Layout F v3 order.)

| Nodes | Port | Data order | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-400 | P1 = line 1 | post 1 up > 72" f1 > rafter 2 up > hop > rafter 3 dn > 72" f2 (3>2) > post 2 dn > bottom hop > post 3 up > 72" f3 > rafter 4 up > hop > rafter 5 dn > 72" f4 (5>4) > post 4 dn | 400 |
| 401-800 | P2 = line 2 | same pattern from post 5 (posts 5-8, rafters 6-9) | 400 |
| 801-1200 | P3 = line 3 | same pattern from post 9 (posts 9-12, rafters 10-12, 1) | 400 |
| 1201-1525 | P4 = line 4 | 36" rail from post 1 counterclockwise (faces 1 to 11, 275) > inject + hop up at post 12 > ring (50) | 325 |

The CSV has a `port` column, and `pos_in_member` is geometric (posts bottom to top, rafters wall to ring, rails post f to f+1, ring CCW from post 1), so reversed units are visible in the CSV.

Member totals: posts 420 (35 each), rafters 480 (40 each), 36" rails 275, 72" rails 300, ring 50 = 1,525.

Node n in `gazebo.xmodel` is row n of `model/gazebo_pixels.csv` (start channel and real x/y/z; origin at the center on the ground, +z up).

## Wiring plan (v4: Kulp K4, one 12 V 360 W supply)
The current plan is [`docs/physical_layout_v4.md`](docs/physical_layout_v4.md). It covers the route, open items to verify, and the power check.

![v4 port diagram](docs/k4_ports_v4_diagram.png)

- **Controller and supply:** a Kulp K4 ("K4-4B" in the plan, assumed K4-PB) and one 12 V 360 W (30 A) supply, in a Bud box (14.73 x 10.79 x 5.69") at the base of post 1.
  Pixels: Wally's Pro 12 V WS2811 X-connect bullets (22 mA/px), in 100-px strings.
- **Lines:** lines 1-3 are 400 px each (4 posts, 4 rafters, 4 top rails; 4 x 100-px strings joined at 2 centre hops and 1 bottom hop). Line 4 is 325 px (11 mid rails + the ring).
- **Injection:** at the end of each of lines 1-3 (post 4, 8 and 12 bases), and on line 4 at post 12 before the ring hop. The optional injection after 200 px (post 2, 6 and 10 bases) is recommended.
  Use 14 AWG leads, fused at the supply.
- **Power:** full white is ~33.5 A / 403 W at the 22 mA spec, so one 30 A supply needs a **~70% brightness limit**, or add a second supply. See the doc for the 55-60 mA case.

| Port | Pixels | Start channel | Universes (170 px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 400 | 1 | 1-3 |
| P2 | 400 | 1201 | 3-5 |
| P3 | 400 | 2401 | 5-8 |
| P4 | 325 | 3601 | 8-9 |

- In FPP, set the K4 pixel outputs as in the table (all WS2811, RGB). The `.fseq` files assume channel 1 = pixel 1 red.
- **History:**
  - [`docs/physical_layout_v3.md`](docs/physical_layout_v3.md) + `docs/k4_ports_diagram.png` are Layout F v3 (K4-PB in a ring box, 4 x LRS-350, 400/375/375/375).
  - `docs/wiring.md` is the original 5-port plan.
  - `docs/physical_layout.md` (Layout B) and `docs/physical_layout_v2.md` (Layout F) are intermediate designs.

## Importing the model into xLights
1. xLights > Layout > Models > **Create a new Custom model** (or the Import Custom Model option) and select `model/gazebo.xmodel`.
2. Check the model is 3D: width 189, height 92, depth 189 (1 cell = 1"), 1,525 nodes. Set the string type to RGB nodes and the start channel as needed.
3. Assign it to the K4 ports per the table above (P1 nodes 1-400, P2 401-800, P3 801-1200, P4 1201-1525).
4. If the import fails or the nodes look wrong, create a 3D custom model with the same width/height/depth and rebuild node positions from `gazebo_pixels.csv`.
5. The `.fseq` files are not editable xLights sequences; use them for FPP playback, or build effects on the model in xLights.

## Playing the sequences on FPP
1. Copy the `sequences/*.fseq` files to FPP's sequences folder (File Manager).
2. Make a playlist; put the sequence you want in it and set it to loop.
3. Make sure the FPP output channel range starts where the model starts and covers 4,575 channels.
4. Format: FSEQ v2, uncompressed, 20 fps (50 ms), 4,575 channels per frame. Single animations are 10 s (first seven) or 20 s (calm seven, loopable).

python scripts/night_cam.py       # night renders

## Size and Git LFS
The tracked content is about 80 MB: sequences ~44 MB, previews ~29 MB, renders and model ~7 MB. GitHub rejects files over 100 MB and warns over 50 MB, so none is a problem alone, but total is over 50 MB. Consider Git LFS for `*.fseq` and `*.gif`:
```
git lfs install
git lfs track "*.fseq" "*.gif"
```
(Git LFS is not currently enabled for this repo.)

## Caveats
- **Not yet verified in xLights.** The `.xmodel` is valid XML in the classic `<custommodel parm1 parm2 Depth CustomModel=...>` format; `CustomModelCompressed` is not used.
- The FSEQ v2 files (32-byte header, uncompressed, 50 ms step) follow the FSEQ v2 format and are parsed by the web preview; playback on FPP still needs to be confirmed.
- A custom model can't store "pixels face inward"; the CSV positions are the real locations. 3D positions are rounded to 1" cells (no two pixels share a cell in the current build).
- Previews and renders come from the scripts in this repo, not xLights. Preview colors will differ from real pixels.
- The ring height (3.5"), 1x4 rail centering, and brace geometry are estimates; verify against the build.
- Brace-pixel exclusion, wiring order and the port plan are design proposals; confirm against the installed controller and wire direction.
